The next Poly Room

A place worth hanging around.

The Clean Side is a separate sequel to Poly Room. The direction is simple: make being together feel good, and give you a space you want to come back to.

01 — THE COMPANY

People come first.

The goal is easy conversation, expressive interactions, and room to hang out without joining a match.

02 — THE PLACE

A little more lived in.

Warmer light, more considered furniture, and inviting spaces are the visual direction for the sequel.

03 — THE MOMENTS

Make your own fun.

Pick things up, pass them around, and improvise with friends. Activities support the hangout.

These describe the sequel’s development goals. Final features and multiplayer support will be listed with each release.
