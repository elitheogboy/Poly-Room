A new chapter

A place worth hanging around.

Poly Room: The Clean Side is a separate sequel to Poly Room. The focus is on social spaces, physical interaction, and giving friends more ways to make their own fun.

01 / THE PEOPLE

Company comes first.

The goal is a place to talk, relax, and spend time together without needing to join a match.

02 / THE SPACES

Make yourself at home.

More inviting environments, warmer lighting, and more detailed spaces are the sequel’s visual direction.

03 / THE MOMENTS

Make your own fun.

Playful objects and hands-on interactions are intended to turn ordinary hangouts into memorable moments.

Before you jump in.

No. Poly Room: The Clean Side is a separate sequel. Downloads on this page should be builds of the sequel.
