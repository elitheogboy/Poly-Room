A New Chapter

A place worth hanging around.

Poly Room: The Clean Side is a standalone sequel designed specifically for social intimacy, physical interaction, and empowering players to create their own sandbox fun.

01 / THE PEOPLE

Company comes first.

A lounge-focused experience. Spend real quality time talking, relaxing, and interacting without the high pressure of immediate gameplay loops.

02 / THE SPACES

Make yourself at home.

Warm ambient lighting, tactile environments, meticulously detailed spaces, and cozy acoustics define the visual atmosphere of this sequel.

03 / THE MOMENTS

Make your own fun.

Interactive physics props, spatial toys, and intuitive hands-on mechanics turn standard hangouts into unpredictable, memorable moments.
